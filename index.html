Why soft is safer

A soft helmet beats a hard one for everyday bumps

Falls are the number-one cause of emergency-room visits for infants and toddlers (CDC). For the low-speed tumbles of learning to walk, soft protection works with your child, not against them.

  • 1

    Absorbs the bump

    Flexible, shock-absorbing foam disperses impact from everyday tumbles far better than a rigid shell.

  • 2

    Comfortable enough to keep on

    Lightweight and breathable — so your baby actually leaves it on instead of pulling it off.

  • 3

    Freedom to explore

    Protects without limiting movement or vision during crawling, cruising and first steps.
