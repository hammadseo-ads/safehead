Our story

Safety that lets little ones be little

SafeheadBABY was created around a simple belief: babies learn by exploring — and parents shouldn't have to hover to keep them safe.

Why we exist

Peace of mind for parents, freedom for babies

Learning to crawl, cruise and walk means a lot of little tumbles. Instead of constantly cruising behind unsteady walkers, SafeheadBABY gives babies the freedom they need — and parents the peace of mind they deserve.

Our patented soft headgear is light, breathable and designed to protect the forehead without getting in the way of a good adventure. It's the reassurance that, when a wobbly step ends in a bump, there's something soft in between.

Grows with your child

From first steps to bigger adventures

The Safehead range grows with your little explorer. SafeheadBABY soft headgear cushions the everyday bumps of learning to walk — then SafeheadTOTS takes over with XS bike helmets and UPF 50+ sun caps for bigger days out.

Same trusted, featherlight protection at every stage — just sized and styled for what comes next.
